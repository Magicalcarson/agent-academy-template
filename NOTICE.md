## Naming and customization

The public template ships only generic, original role-based identities: Academy Lead, Academy Deputy, Academy Analyst, Academy Challenger, and Academy Steward. No third-party franchise names, character personas, logos, artwork, audio, or other branded assets are part of the default template.

Operators may freely replace display names and persona text with their own original cast. Permanent member ids and paths are compatibility keys; changing those ids after installation requires a deliberate migration rather than a display-name edit.
