# Staff Workflow Research

Use this skill before designing screens, procedures, or automation for recurring operational work.

## Research approach

1. Identify roles, goals, frequency, location, tools, inputs, outputs, and time pressure.
2. Observe or elicit the actual workflow rather than relying only on the official process.
3. Trace handoffs, approvals, queues, duplicate entry, interruptions, and context switching.
4. Ask where errors occur, which workarounds exist, and why they remain useful.
5. Examine exception and recovery paths, including incomplete inputs, corrections, conflicting records, and unavailable approvers.
6. Identify the evidence people rely on, who is trusted to change records, and what must be logged.
7. Validate findings with more than one relevant perspective when feasible, and label inference separately from observation.

Avoid automating a process until the research explains routine work, meaningful exceptions, and the cost of a mistaken action. Protect participant and customer data in notes and artifacts.
