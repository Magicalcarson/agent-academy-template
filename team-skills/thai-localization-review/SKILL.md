# Thai Localization Review

Use this skill to review Thai or bilingual interface text, forms, documents, notices, errors, empty states, and help content.

## Workflow

1. Establish audience, context, desired tone, source meaning, terminology authority, and space constraints.
2. Check semantic parity across languages, including negation, conditions, dates, quantities, status, and action consequences.
3. Prefer natural Thai over word-for-word translation while preserving the original requirement.
4. Keep action labels specific, errors calm and recoverable, and notices clear about the next step.
5. Maintain a consistent glossary for domain terms, names, abbreviations, units, numerals, dates, currency, and formality.
6. Inspect Thai-script line height, wrapping, truncation, mixed-script spacing, table width, font support, zoom, and contrast.
7. Report uncertainty and request a subject-matter decision when wording could change a legal, financial, safety, or privacy meaning.

Use fabricated placeholders in examples. Do not place real personal, financial, or contractual content into localization examples or external tools.
